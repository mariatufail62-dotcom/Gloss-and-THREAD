# Editorial roadmap: the first 60 articles

**For:** the Gloss & Thread editors planning launch content.

Three launch pillars: **Nails (20)**, **Outfits (20)**, **Shoes (20)**.

## How to use this plan

- **Keyword candidates are unverified.** No search volume, difficulty or trend figures are given, because none were measured. Before you commission an article, check demand and the current results in a real tool (Google Search Console once live, Google Trends, Keyword Planner, or a paid SEO tool), and record the source and date in the brief.
- **Nothing here is published automatically.** Every article goes through the CMS workflow: draft → editor review (fact-check, sources, image rights) → **Approved** → publish. The CMS blocks publishing until review is approved.
- **Before writing,** search the keyword, read the top results and note what they miss. Plan something more useful: clearer steps, a comparison table, real outfit combinations, or photography that actually shows the detail.
- **Never fabricate** testing, quotes, celebrity references, statistics or expert review. If an article needs expert input (nail health, foot health), interview a credentialed professional and name them with permission.
- **Imagery** must be original, commissioned or properly licensed, with credit and alt text recorded in the media library.

Each entry lists:

- **Intent:** what the searcher wants.
- **Keyword:** the primary keyword candidate.
- **Format:** the CMS content type.
- **Questions:** supporting questions to answer.
- **Links:** suggested internal links.
- **Images:** imagery requirements.

Links that point to articles not yet written are marked *(planned)*.

## Publishing cadence

| Phase | Articles | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch | 9 (3 per pillar) | Makes each pillar category page indexable (threshold set in Admin → Categories) |
| Months 1–2 | 2–3 per week | Prioritize how-tos and explainers that build internal links |
| Months 3–4 | Remaining topics | Add subcategory content once a subcategory has 3+ articles |

Review each article at least once a year. When an update is meaningful, tick "Meaningful update" in the editor so readers see the updated date.
